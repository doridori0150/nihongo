## REQUEST (for Codex)

### Goal
Round 4 of original character art for a Japanese-learning web app themed around a high-school anime club. Add two NEW first-year members in exactly the approved series style.

### Approved style references (attached images)
- `docs/art/ref/shizuku.png`, `minato.png`, `hiyori.png`, `ritsu.png` — all approved by the user. Match them closely: same line weight and lineart color, same flat cel shading with one soft shadow tone, same eye rendering, same color saturation, same bust-up framing and camera distance, same flat pastel background treatment, same school uniform (navy blazer with pale piping, white shirt, colored ribbon/tie).
- New characters must look like they belong to the same series as these images.

### Characters to draw

1. `akane` — 1st-year girl, a bright "gyaru" on the outside, secretly a hardcore robot-anime and plastic-model otaku.
   - Look: long wavy light-brown/honey-blonde hair, confident bright eyes with emphasized lashes, small earrings, light makeup, nails with simple colorful nail art. Oversized cream or pastel cardigan over the uniform (or tied around the waist), loosened orange ribbon (#e0782b), shirt sleeves pushed up.
   - Otaku hints (original designs only): a hairpin shaped like a mecha head antenna (V-shaped fin), a keychain made from plastic-model runner parts, an invented robot pin badge on the cardigan, model-kit nippers in her hand OR holding a small unbranded model kit box with an original robot illustration (no text).
   - Expression: variant 1 sunny gyaru smile with a peace sign; variant 2 sparkling, excited "otaku mode" face, leaning in while showing the model kit.
   - Wholesome: fully clothed uniform, normal skirt length, no fan-service.
2. `keita` — 1st-year boy, 190 cm tall, very quiet cosplayer whose family runs a tailor shop.
   - Look: tall, broad shoulders filling the frame, slightly hunched as if used to ducking; short dark hair with slightly long bangs; calm, gentle, sleepy-looking eyes; expressionless or a tiny shy smile. Neat navy blazer (well-tailored), dark green tie (#3f7d5c).
   - Props: a yellow tailor's measuring tape draped around his neck, a pincushion band on his wrist, holding a piece of costume fabric or a needle and thread. Big hands, drawn correctly.
   - Expression: variant 1 quiet, looking slightly down at the viewer, a small polite nod ("…っす"); variant 2 holding up a half-finished costume piece with a small proud smile, ears a little red.
   - Should feel gentle, not intimidating or delinquent.

### Constraints
- Work only in `docs/art/characters/r4/`. Do not modify other folders.
- Original characters only. Do not reproduce, trace or name any existing character or mecha (no Gundam or other real robot designs, no brand logos, no text on boxes or badges).
- High-school students: fully clothed school uniform, wholesome framing, no sexualized poses or expressions.
- Faces must stay readable when cropped to a circle and shown at 48px: face large, near the upper middle, simple background.
- Avoid: glossy generic "AI anime" look, plastic skin, painterly texture, heavy gradients, malformed hands, a style that drifts away from the approved references.

Finish with a short Korean summary.
