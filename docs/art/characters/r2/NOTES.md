# Character art R2

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool, one call per image. Approved attached shizuku.png and minato.png supplied in the conversation as style references. Reference files were not modified.

## Delivery and review

- Six portraits exported as 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG. Tool originals were square 1254 × 1254; delivery copies resized with Lanczos.
- contact_sheet.png: one row, shizuku.png, minato.png, hiyori_1.png, hiyori_2.png, ritsu_1.png, ritsu_2.png, saeko_1.png, saeko_2.png. Labels are outside the artwork.
- Hiyori: covered laugh / pointing, half-lidded stare, raised chin and fang; Ritsu: reserved / half-smile with tie adjustment; Saeko: papers / clearly visible lollipop.

## Limitations and remaining differences

- Exact style equality is not guaranteed. New portraits are more muted and flatter than the approved references, with fewer eye highlights; Ritsu and Saeko backgrounds are darker than the requested pale pastel treatment and have slight tonal variation.
- Variant details are not perfectly locked: Hiyori star pin and capsule inserts differ; Ritsu choker and ear studs differ. These are alternative designs, not production continuity sheets.
- Ritsu's untucked shirt hem cannot be verified in bust-up framing.
- 48px faces remain distinguishable, but fine details such as the fang, safety pins and capsule are not readable at that scale. Face-centered cropping is preferable for avatars.
