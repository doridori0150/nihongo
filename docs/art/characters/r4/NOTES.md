# R4 character art

- Generated using the built-in image_gen tool; no CLI/API fallback.
- Style inputs for all four images: docs/art/ref/shizuku.png, minato.png, hiyori.png, ritsu.png.
- Variant 2 additionally uses the corresponding generated variant 1 as an identity reference.
- Final portraits: 1024 x 1024 RGB PNG. Generated square images were resized with Lanczos for the exact delivery dimensions; no cropping or content retouching.
- Contact sheet: one row in the requested order, with filenames below each image.

## Review and limitations

- Original robot imagery, school uniforms, requested background colors, distinct poses and hobby props are present; no visible lettering, logos, or watermark in the portraits.
- Visual style is matched by reference, not guaranteed pixel-identical. Generated backgrounds retain subtle tonal variation rather than a perfectly uniform fill.
- Akane's cardigan cuffs remain near the wrists rather than clearly pushed up to the forearms. Her variant 1 is a winking sunny smile. Small robot/keychain shapes differ slightly between variants.
- Keita's height is suggested by broad shoulders and viewpoint; a bust portrait cannot demonstrate an exact 190 cm height. His two smiles differ subtly, with the costume display and ear blush distinguishing variant 2.
- Exact ribbon/tie hex colors were provided as prompts, not enforced by recoloring shaded pixels.
- Facial readability was checked in 48 px circular previews. Tiny hobby accessories are not expected to remain legible at that scale.
