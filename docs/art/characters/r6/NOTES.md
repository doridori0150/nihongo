## Validation and limitations

- Four portraits exported as 1024 x 1024 RGB PNG; generated source images resampled with Lanczos only, with no repainting.
- Contact sheet: one row, hiyori.png, akane.png, keita.png, mei_1.png, mei_2.png, sota_1.png, sota_2.png; filename labels below.
- Original references were read only. Project writes restricted to this r6 folder.
- Visual review: consistent character identities, uniform, required props and pose variants; no visible lettering, logos, or watermarks. Mei's badges and open-notebook writing pose were corrected with image_gen.
- Style matching is visual, not an exact reproduction guarantee. Backgrounds retain slight tonal variation instead of mathematically uniform solid fills; ribbon/tie hex colors are prompt targets, not exact pixel colors.
- Sota's second notebook is illustrated as a small bound notebook rather than the spiral notebook in his first variant; same character and functional prop, with different binding.
- 48px circular full-image previews are provided in avatar_check.png for inspection; props naturally lose detail at that size.
