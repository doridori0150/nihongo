## REQUEST (for Codex)

### Goal
One lively key-visual illustration of the five approved characters hanging out together in their club room, for the top of the app's home screen.

### References (attached)
- `docs/art/ref/clubroom.png` — the approved club room background. Use this room (layout, furniture, colors).
- `docs/art/ref/minato.png`, `shizuku.png`, `hiyori.png`, `ritsu.png`, `saeko.png` — approved character designs. Keep faces, hair, outfits and props exactly.
- `docs/art/ref/clubroom_mood.webp` — user's MOOD reference only: a crowded club room where every member is doing their own thing at once, candid and funny, slightly high camera angle. Do not copy its characters, composition details or any artwork in it.

### Scene
After school in club room 305, everyone in one frame, each acting in character:
- Saeko (advisor teacher) slouched in the doorway or on the sofa with canned coffee, looking tired and done with everything.
- Minato (president) at the whiteboard pushing up his glasses, explaining something no one is listening to.
- Shizuku hugging her doujinshi at the long table, peeking shyly with a small blush.
- Ritsu sitting on the table edge tuning her bass, cool and unbothered.
- Hiyori leaning toward the viewer, pointing and grinning smugly as if teasing "senpai" (the viewer).
- Leave a little empty space at the table as if a seat is waiting for the viewer (the player).

- `docs/art/key/clubroom_all_tall.png` — portrait 1024×1536 version (for phones), same scene reframed.
