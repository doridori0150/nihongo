# Y3 sprite delivery notes

Built-in image_gen was used for all eight images (one call per asset). No CLI/API fallback, recoloring, background removal, or alpha normalization was used. Generated alpha was preserved. Project writes are confined to docs/art/sprites/.

## References and interpretation

- Mei and Sota approved portraits were used as identity references; akane_normal and hiyori_smug as style and framing references.
- Each expression was generated by editing its own character normal sprite. Torso/hip/leg stance is maintained visually; requested writing, raised notebook, and cheek scratching require arm changes.
- To honor no text, Mei is shown with the pen at the notebook page without readable writing. Sota retains the fan and notebook in his shy variant by holding both together.

## Verification

- All 34 pre-existing files in the sprite directory retain their original SHA-256 hashes.
- All eight sprites are 1024 x 1536 RGBA PNGs with real transparency. No green fallback was necessary.
- Corners below are listed TL, TR, BL, BR. Alpha extrema are 0–254 for every image: the generated character interiors are almost opaque, not exactly 255. This was preserved without modification.
- contact_sheet_y3.png composites the eight new images and akane_normal onto flat RGB (128,128,128), with file-name labels. Visual inspection confirmed the transparent backdrop, distinct expressions, retained props and consistent standing composition.

## Limitations / not exactly satisfied

- Top margin is tighter than requested: Mei normal/surprised/sad have hair touching the top edge; Mei happy has about 3 px and Sota variants about 5–6 px to the main visible hair (alpha > 128). A uniform small clear head margin was not achieved.
- Generative edits are visually consistent rather than pixel-identical. Small line, hair, cloth and accessory details may vary; exact style or geometry equality is not claimed.
- Faint near-transparent pixels extend beyond the main silhouette in some files. Corner alpha is exactly zero and the mid-grey composite was inspected, but no manual matte cleanup was performed.
