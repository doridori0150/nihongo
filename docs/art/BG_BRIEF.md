## REQUEST (for Codex)

### Goal
Create a set of 15 original visual-novel background illustrations (no people) for a Japanese-learning visual novel set in a Japanese high school anime club.

### References (attached)
- `docs/art/ref/shizuku.png`, `minato.png`, `hiyori.png`, `ritsu.png`, `saeko.png` — the approved character art. Backgrounds must match their clean anime look (crisp lines, flat cel colors with soft lighting) so characters can be layered on top.
- `docs/art/ref/clubroom_mood.webp` — user-provided MOOD reference only for the club room: a cramped, lived-in otaku club room crowded with bookshelves of manga and DVDs, a whiteboard with notes, a long table with papers, posters, window light. Use it for atmosphere; do not copy its layout, characters, posters or any recognizable artwork.

### Backgrounds (file name = key)
1. `clubroom` — club room 305 in an old club building, afternoon. Long table, worn sofa, bookshelves full of manga/DVDs/setting books, figure display case by the window, whiteboard with doodles and a schedule, mini fridge, posters of ORIGINAL made-up anime (no real titles/logos, no readable text).
2. `clubroom_evening` — same room at dusk, orange sunset light, lamps on.
3. `classroom` — Japanese high-school classroom, daytime, cherry blossoms faintly visible outside.
4. `hallway` — school corridor with windows and club recruitment flyers (no readable text).
5. `school_gate` — school front gate with full-bloom cherry blossoms, April morning.
6. `rooftop` — school rooftop with fence, blue sky.
7. `station` — suburban train station platform, daytime.
8. `akihabara` — Akihabara-like electric town street with signs and anime billboards (make every sign and billboard original/illegible; no real shop names or logos).
9. `shopping_street` — retro covered shopping arcade (shotengai), evening.
10. `convenience_store` — interior of a Japanese convenience store at night (generic, no real brand).
11. `beach_inn` — seaside Japanese inn (minshuku) with tatami room opening to the sea, summer.
12. `festival_night` — summer festival night with lanterns, food stalls and fireworks in the sky.
13. `comiket` — huge exhibition hall exterior with a long line of people seen from far away (people as tiny silhouettes only), early morning summer.
14. `shrine_winter` — Shinto shrine at New Year with light snow, torii, lanterns.
15. `apartment` — small one-room apartment of a student living alone (desk, futon, anime posters, Korean snacks), night.

### Format
- Landscape PNG 1536×1024, saved as `docs/art/bg/<key>.png`.
- No people (except tiny distant silhouettes in `comiket`), no text, no watermarks, no real logos or brands.
- Leave the lower third and the center reasonably uncluttered so characters and a dialogue box can sit on top.
- `docs/art/bg/contact_sheet.png` — all 15 as thumbnails with file names.
- `docs/art/bg/NOTES.md` — prompt per image and anything you could not satisfy.

### Constraints
- Work only in `docs/art/bg/`.
- Consistent lighting/color treatment across the set.

Finish with a short Korean summary.
