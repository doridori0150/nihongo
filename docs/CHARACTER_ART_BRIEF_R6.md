## REQUEST (for Codex)

### Approved style references (attached images)

### Characters to draw
1. `mei` — 1st-year girl, a one-year exchange student from Taiwan. Bright, earnest and curious; loves VTubers and dreams of being a bilingual streamer; still learning Japanese and writes everything down.
   - Look: shoulder-length dark-brown hair with one thin side braid, warm brown eyes, friendly open expression. Uniform with a red-pink ribbon (#d94f6b). Over-ear headphones resting around her neck, a small spiral notebook bristling with colorful sticky notes, a pen. A couple of pin badges of an ORIGINAL invented VTuber mascot on her bag strap.
   - No national-costume or stereotyped hairstyles (no buns, no qipao); she is simply a high-school girl defined by her hobbies.
   - Variant 1: cheerful wave hello, notebook in the other hand. Variant 2: intently writing in the notebook, eyes sparkling as if she just learned a new word.
2. `sota` — 1st-year boy who transferred from Osaka; the loud funny guy (the "boke") who dreams of writing light novels.
   - Look: average height, slim, messy light-brown hair, lively eyes, big grin. Blazer worn open, blue tie (#3a6fb5) slightly loose. Props: a folded paper fan (harisen) for comedy and a pocket notebook with a pen behind his ear.
   - Variant 1: big grin, playfully pointing the harisen at the viewer as if delivering a punchline. Variant 2: scribbling story ideas in the notebook with a proud "I've got it!" look.
   - Cheerful and kind, not a delinquent.
