## REQUEST (for Codex)

### Goal
Redesign one character, `keita`, in exactly the approved series style.

### Approved style references (attached images)
- `docs/art/ref/shizuku.png`, `hiyori.png`, `ritsu.png`, `akane.png` — all approved. Match them closely: same line weight and lineart color, same flat cel shading with one soft shadow tone, same eye rendering, same color saturation, same bust-up framing and camera distance, same flat pastel background treatment, same school uniform (navy blazer with pale piping, white shirt, colored tie).

### Character
`keita` — 1st-year high-school BOY (16), small for his age (about 150 cm) with a cute, youthful face; people mistake him for a middle schooler, which he hates.
- Look: small frame, round face, large calm eyes, soft fluffy dark-brown hair with a slight cowlick, a little sleepy/deadpan look. Navy blazer one size too big so the sleeves reach his knuckles, dark green tie (#3f7d5c) neatly tied.
- Props: a yellow tailor's measuring tape draped around his neck (long on his small frame), a pincushion band on his wrist, holding costume fabric or a needle and thread.
- The charm is the GAP: cute looks, curt and stoic attitude ("…っす").
- Variant 1: deadpan, slightly pouting face looking straight at the viewer, holding fabric to his chest.
- Variant 2: annoyed, cheeks a little pink, looking away as if someone just called him cute, holding up a half-finished costume piece.

### Deliverables (exact paths)
- `docs/art/characters/r5/keita_1.png`, `keita_2.png` — square 1024×1024 PNG, bust-up, plain soft green pastel background, no text, no watermark.
- `docs/art/characters/r5/contact_sheet.png` — one row: approved shizuku, hiyori, akane first, then the two new images, file name under each.
- `docs/art/characters/r5/NOTES.md` — prompt used for each image and anything you could not satisfy.

### Constraints
- Work only in `docs/art/characters/r5/`. Do not modify other folders.
- Original character only; do not reproduce or name any existing character.
- Wholesome: a fully clothed high-school student, youthful but clearly a teenager, not a small child. No suggestive pose or expression, no fan-service, nothing that sexualizes youthfulness.
- Faces must stay readable when cropped to a circle and shown at 48px.
- Avoid: glossy generic "AI anime" look, plastic skin, painterly texture, heavy gradients, malformed hands, style drift.

Finish with a short Korean summary.
