## REQUEST (for Codex)

### Goal
Round 2 of original mascot art for a Japanese-learning web app themed around a high-school anime club. Match the APPROVED style exactly and redesign/add three characters.

### Approved style references (attached images)
- `docs/art/ref/shizuku.png` and `docs/art/ref/minato.png` are approved by the user. Match them closely: same line weight and lineart color, same flat cel shading with one soft shadow tone, same eye rendering, same color saturation, same bust-up framing and camera distance, same flat pastel background treatment, same uniform design (navy blazer, white shirt, colored ribbon/tie).
- The user said: "character style like Shizuku". New characters must look like they belong to the same series as these two images.

### Characters to draw

1. `hiyori` — 1st-year girl, the cheeky "mesugaki"-type kouhai (REDESIGN; the previous version was rejected for looking like a sweet, cheerful girl).
   - Personality: always teasing and looking down on her senpai (the viewer), cocky and mocking, but harmless and funny.
   - Face (most important): half-lidded smug "jito-me" eyes looking DOWN at the viewer, one eyebrow arched / brows tilted in a mocking way, a sly lopsided grin showing one small snaggletooth fang (yaeba). Condescending smug expression, like she is about to say "ざぁこ" (loser) to her senpai. NOT a wink, NOT an innocent happy smile.
   - Pose options: hand half-covering her mouth stifling a mocking laugh ("ぷぷっ"), or pointing at the viewer and smirking, chin slightly raised.
   - Look: petite, pink twin-tails, yellow star hairpin, pink ribbon (#e86a9a), blazer sleeves a bit long, gacha-capsule keychain on bag.
   - Keep it wholesome: fully clothed uniform, no blush-lewd expression, no heart-shaped pupils, no suggestive pose, no fan-service. Comedic bratty, not sexual.
2. `ritsu` — 2nd-year girl, boyish cool punk rocker (NEW; inspired by the vibe of a punk-band heroine, but an original character).
   - Short messy black hair (boyish cut, ear-length), sharp eyes with subtle dark smoky eyeliner, cool slightly bored expression or a confident half-smile.
   - Punk accessories over the school uniform: black choker, studded wristbands, a safety pin or two on the blazer lapel, loosened tie in dark red, shirt untucked; bass guitar case strap over one shoulder.
   - Boyish but clearly a girl; calm, cool, few words. No brand logos (no designer emblems), no text.
3. `saeko` — the club's advisor teacher, mature adult woman around 30 (NEW; vibe of a strong, cool mentor who is rough-tongued but warm).
   - Striking, confident adult beauty with long dark wine-red hair loosely tied, sharp knowing eyes, an amused half-smile (looks like she is about to tease the students and then help them).
   - Teacher outfit: white blouse with a loosened neck, dark cardigan or tailored jacket, ID lanyard; holding a lollipop stick like a cigarette (comedic, no actual cigarette) OR a stack of student papers.
   - Clearly an adult (taller proportions, mature face) but modest, professional clothing; no emphasis on body, no fan-service.
